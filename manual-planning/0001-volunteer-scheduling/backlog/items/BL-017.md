# BL-017 — TeamLeader scheduling entry and resource-scoped capabilities

> ## 📌 Rank 3 · Wave 1 (tier 1) · Issue [#20](https://github.com/tiagoluizpoli/church/issues/20)
>
> **Why it sits here** — TeamLeaders have real, team-scoped scheduling authority but no usable Scheduling entry. The current nav probe admits only ChurchAdmins and Ministry leaders, so a TeamLeader-only caller is excluded before reaching the roster work they may manage.

**Status**: Ranked — wave 1, rank 3
**Feature area**: Scheduling Authorization / Navigation

## Summary

Make `/scheduling` a server-backed capability index. It must expose entries only for the caller's authorized Church, Ministry, and Team scopes, so a TeamLeader can roster their led Teams without receiving Ministry-wide authority.

## Full Context

`sub_leader` is retired. Its successor is **TeamLeader**: a Team Membership at `leader`, constrained to explicitly led Teams. A person can lead a Ministry in one area and lead only a Team in another, so selecting a global highest role would over-grant the Ministry-management surface.

The current `useCallerRoles` hook infers one global boolean from `listMinistries()`. Its backing `hasSchedulingAccess` evaluates Church and Ministry authority only, while the Ministry list itself contains only led Ministries. Neither represents TeamLeader scope. Existing route relocation and the `AuthorityService` seam have already landed, so BL-021 and BL-011 are no longer blockers.

The confirmed policy is recorded in [ADR-0004](../../../../docs/adr/0004-resource-scoped-scheduling-capabilities.md) and [Spec 025](../../../../specs/025-teamleader-scheduling-capabilities/spec.md). A later end-to-end workflow review may refine the screen composition, but does not alter these resource boundaries.

## Suggested Approach

1. Project the caller's permitted Scheduling entries server-side from `AuthorityService`: Church planning, led Ministries, and led Teams. This projection is navigation data only; it does not replace resource-level authorization.
2. Replace the reactive-403 nav probe with the projection and make `/scheduling` its capability index. Suppress Team entries covered by the caller's Ministry leadership in that Ministry.
3. Route a TeamLeader only to Team-scoped roster work. They may view their Team's needs and Volunteers and assign/unassign in `rostering`; published/past work is read-only. They cannot tailor, publish, plan, or view other Teams' roster details.
4. Enforce direct URLs by resource scope without disclosure: callers with some Scheduling access return to the index; callers with none return to Dashboard. Keep server authorization as the final boundary.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- No blocking backlog item. `AuthorityService` and the authenticated route tree already provide the required seams.
- A subsequent workflow review may redesign the screen flow, but must preserve the capability and scope contract above.

## Success Criteria

1. The client consumes a server-produced, resource-scoped Scheduling capability projection; it no longer infers access from a failed or successful data request.
2. The capability index shows Church planning only to ChurchAdmins; Ministry entries only for led Ministries; and Team-roster entries only for otherwise-unavailable led Teams.
3. A TeamLeader can see and manage assignments only for led Teams during `rostering`, sees current/future locked rosters, and sees published/past rosters read-only.
4. TeamLeaders cannot access Ministry tailoring, planning, publishing, other Teams' roster details, or any resource outside a led Team. Wrong-scope deep links disclose nothing.
5. A capability-loading failure does not imply access: nav remains hidden while resolving and the index presents retryable failure UI.
6. Server, contract, and Playwright tests cover ChurchAdmin without a Volunteer profile, Ministry leader, TeamLeader-only, mixed MinistryLeader/TeamLeader across different Ministries, plain Volunteer, wrong-scope deep links, and revoked leadership.
