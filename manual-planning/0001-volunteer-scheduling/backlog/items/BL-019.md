# BL-019 — Volunteer response health report for leaders

**Status**: Backlog
**Feature area**: Volunteer Scheduling / Leader Operations

## Summary

Give ministry leaders a report showing volunteer response patterns across recent planning cycles so they can follow up with people who repeatedly do not respond, intentionally decline, or appear to have stopped participating.

## Full Context

This was identified while defining Cycle board recommendation ranking and is intentionally outside the Event Builder MVP. The report should distinguish pending or missing responses from explicit declines and other response states, show useful trends over a configurable period such as the last three cycles or three months, and support leader action rather than merely presenting a ranking.

The purpose is pastoral and operational: a repeated non-response may mean a volunteer needs a reminder, a conversation, updated expectations, or removal from active planning. The system should avoid treating the report as a judgment score.

## Suggested Approach

Start with a leader-facing response-health view scoped to a ministry, with filters for planning-cycle range, response state, and team. Show the underlying response counts and recent examples so leaders can understand why a person appears. Add explicit follow-up actions only after the report shape and notification/privacy boundaries are decided.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Stable assignment response and availability-check history across planning cycles.
- A decision about retention period and leader access to response history.
- A decision about which follow-up actions belong in the product.

## Success Criteria

1. Leaders can identify repeated non-response separately from repeated decline.
2. The view explains the underlying response history instead of presenting an opaque score.
3. The report supports an intentional follow-up workflow without changing assignment or recommendation behavior.
