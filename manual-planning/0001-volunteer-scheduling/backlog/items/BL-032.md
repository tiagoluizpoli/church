## Summary

Make every Assignment identify the exact SlotRequirement it fills, and allow same-Role requirements within one Shift to remain distinct when Team is not the right discriminator. A short requirement-level label or equivalent concept must represent temporary service posts such as `Templo` and `Kids` without inventing permanent Teams or duplicate Roles.

## Full Context

`SlotRequirement` currently carries Shift, Role, optional Team, required count, and notes. `Assignment` carries Shift, Volunteer, and Role but no requirement or Team reference. Server and builder paths consequently associate or key assignments by Shift plus Role in several places.

That shape cannot faithfully represent two real cases:

- Kids needs one `Líder` requirement for Team Kids and another `Líder` requirement for Team Maternal in the same Shift.
- Projeção uses one Role, `Operador de Projeção`, but may need one operator at `Templo` and one at `Kids`; operators rotate, so those placements are not permanent Teams or different qualifications.

The seed-system decision ([#320](https://github.com/tiagoluizpoli/church/issues/320)) accepts a temporary Projection approximation: one requirement with headcount `2` and notes `1 Templo, 1 Kids`. That workaround must not become the permanent domain model.

## Suggested Approach

Use the dedicated grilling session to settle, rather than assume:

1. Whether Assignment references `slotRequirementId` directly or reaches the requirement through another invariant-preserving relation.
2. The canonical term and shape for a labelled temporary post when Team is absent or inappropriate.
3. How Role qualification, Team membership, TeamLeader authorization, requirement counts, replacements, audits, and assignment uniqueness validate against the selected requirement.
4. How APIs and the builder identify rows by requirement identity instead of collapsing same-Role requirements.
5. Migration/backfill behavior and compatibility with existing historical Assignments.

Do not solve the problem with generated permanent Teams, duplicated Roles, or free-text notes alone.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- A dedicated grilling/domain-modeling session and written specification are required before implementation.
- The seed-system work is not blocked: it uses the documented approximation and later adopts this capability.
- Preserve existing Church, Ministry, Shift, and tenant isolation invariants.

## Success Criteria

1. An Assignment unambiguously fills exactly one SlotRequirement.
2. Two same-Role requirements in one Shift can track staffing independently.
3. Team-scoped requirements continue to enforce Team Membership and TeamLeader authority correctly.
4. A non-Team temporary post such as `Templo` or `Kids` is visible and assignable without creating a permanent Team or duplicate Role.
5. Staffing completion, eligibility, replacement, audit, API, and UI behavior use requirement identity consistently.
6. Existing historical assignments retain a defined interpretation after migration.
