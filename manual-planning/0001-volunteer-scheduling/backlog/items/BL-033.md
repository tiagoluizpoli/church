# BL-033 — Teams: groups of Volunteers that serve and are led as a unit

> ## 🅿️ Parked 2026-10-08 — not queued, no rank
>
> The MVP Journeys map decided that **Sectors**, not Teams, discriminate staffing requirements ([Teams vs. Roles for physical places](https://github.com/tiagoluizpoli/church/issues/368), [ADR-0007](../../../../docs/adr/0007-sectors-not-teams-discriminate-requirements.md)). Every MVP case turned out to be a spot or duty, not a group of people. Real group needs exist — mainly worship bands — but a leader can schedule a band by hand today, so nothing is blocked.
>
> **Promotion trigger** — a Ministry leader asks, for the **second** time, to schedule or lead a fixed group of Volunteers as one unit (a band that rotates together, a sub-leader who staffs only their group), **or** a Flow is planned that needs a Volunteer-to-group relationship Sectors cannot express. On promotion, open a Wayfinder map or grilling session before any spec.

**Status**: Parked
**Feature area**: Scheduling / Ministry Structure

## Summary

Bring Teams back as a real feature: a named group of Volunteers inside one Ministry that can be staffed as a unit and led by a TeamLeader with authority scoped to that group. The domain vocabulary (Team, Team Membership, TeamLeader) is still in [`CONTEXT.md`](../../../../CONTEXT.md); this item is the work to make it usable end to end.

## Full Context

### Why Teams were parked

Charting the MVP surfaced two different needs that the word "team" had been covering:

1. **Spots or duties inside a Ministry** — Babies Room vs. Kids Room in Kids; Main Door, Side Door, Visitor Reception for ushers; Templo vs. Kids for one `Operador de Projeção`. The same qualified people rotate between them; nobody "belongs" to a spot. This became the **Sector** (per-Ministry registry, mandatory on every SlotRequirement, eligibility by Role only). See ADR-0007.
2. **Groups of people** — a worship band (vocalists, guitarist, drummer) that usually serves together and rotates as a block; a group with its own sub-leader. This is what a Team really is, and no MVP Flow needs it. The map already rules **TeamLeader authority beyond a Volunteer's** out of scope.

### Requests heard so far

- Worship: bands that should rotate as a unit ("Band A this Sunday, Band B next"). The product owner judged it **probably needed but not blocking**: the band leader can build the schedule by hand, one Assignment at a time.
- Team-scoped leaders who staff only their group (the TeamLeader concept delivered through BL-017).

### What already exists (as of `develop` @ `54b0ee7`)

Database (`packages/db/src/schema/`):
- `team` (church, ministry, name) — `core.ts`.
- `ministry_volunteer_team` — flat many-to-many Team Membership with `access_level` `member` | `leader` (TeamLeader is a row at `leader`) — `core.ts`.
- `slot_requirement.team_id` (nullable) — `scheduling.ts`. Unlinked from requirements by the Sector work; the column and rows stay.
- `ServingProfileHeadcount.teamId?` — `planning.ts`.
- `assignment` has **no** team column; the Team an Assignment counted for was derived at read time.

Server (`apps/server/src/`):
- Claim algorithm deciding which Team-scoped requirement an Assignment satisfies when a Volunteer belongs to several Teams — `application/db-volunteer-manager.ts` (~line 108–140).
- `TeamRosterMutationPolicy` — a TeamLeader may mutate only while the participation is `rostering`, the Event is `scheduled` and in the future, the requirement is scoped to their single Team, and the Volunteer belongs to it — `domain/authority/team-roster-mutation-policy.ts`.
- `authorityGuard.canManageTeam` and `GET /teams/:teamId/cycles-summary` (TeamLeader's read-only roster cycles) — `api/controllers/rostering-controller.ts`, `application/db-authority-manager.ts`.
- `TeamRepository.listByIds` only — **no endpoint creates, renames, or archives a Team, and none adds or removes Team Members.** Ministry Invitations never grant Team Membership.

Web (`apps/web/src/`):
- TeamLeader roster route `scheduling/rostering/$ministryId/index.tsx` (`?teamId=`) and the read-only team mode of the cycle builder (`cycle-builder-matrix.tsx` `teamId` prop).
- Team chips/badges in builder and volunteer components.

Seeds: Teams appear in `apps/server/seeds/blueprints/` (`minimal-church.ts`, `history.ts`, `directory/cross-ministry.ts`), `builders/volunteer.ts`, and several `e2e/recipes/`. The MVP disables their use on requirements; rows may remain.

Disposition of this code for the MVP (park behind a flag vs. leave dormant) is decided by the MVP map ticket [Dispositions for existing features](https://github.com/tiagoluizpoli/church/issues/386).

## Suggested Approach

Open a grilling/domain-modeling session first; settle, rather than assume:

1. **What a Team is for scheduling** — a shortcut that places all its members at once, a filter on the staffing board, an eligibility restriction, or only an authority boundary? Bands suggest "place the whole group into one Shift, each member under their own Role and Sector".
2. **Relationship to Sectors** — is a Team ever tied to a Sector, or orthogonal? Keep ADR-0007's rule that Sectors are spots and Teams are people.
3. **Team Membership management** — who creates Teams and adds members (ChurchAdmin, Ministry leader, TeamLeader?), and whether invitations may grant Team Membership.
4. **TeamLeader authority** — keep the existing `TeamRosterMutationPolicy` shape or redesign it now that requirements are Sector-scoped and Assignments fill one SlotRequirement.
5. **Availability** — whether a band answers availability as a group or each member separately (today: one AvailabilityCheck per Ministry membership).
6. **Data** — reuse `team` / `ministry_volunteer_team`, and drop or repurpose `slot_requirement.team_id` and `ServingProfileHeadcount.teamId`.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- The MVP Sector work (Assignment fills exactly one SlotRequirement; SlotRequirement = Shift + Sector + Role + headcount) must have landed — Teams build on that shape.
- ADR-0007 constrains the design: a Team must not replace Sectors as the requirement discriminator.

## Success Criteria

1. A Ministry leader can create a Team, add and remove members, and see who belongs to it, without database access.
2. The agreed scheduling behaviour for a Team (e.g. placing a band into a Shift in one action) works on the staffing board and respects one-Assignment-per-Volunteer-per-Shift.
3. TeamLeader authority is defined against Sector-scoped requirements and enforced server-side.
4. Dormant Team columns either carry the new meaning or are removed, with no ambiguity left in the schema.
